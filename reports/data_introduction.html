1. Overview

The project compares Alzheimer's disease (AD) with cognitively normal controls (CN) in two modalities. Structural MRI comes from ADNI as FreeSurfer-derived regional measures (no images are used yet). Resting-state EEG comes from two independent public datasets, BrainLat (Latin America, two sites) and OpenNeuro ds004504 (Greece, one site), processed with the same pipeline. The labels are consistent across all three: dx = AD / CN, sex = M / F.

3. MRI: ADNI structural measures

Source tables: All_Subjects_UCSFFSX7 (UCSF FreeSurfer 7 cross-sectional runs), DXSUM (diagnosis), PTDEMOG (demographics), DATADIC and Key_MRI. One scan per subject (the earliest that passes QC), 3T only, diagnosis from the closest DXSUM visit (median gap 10 days). Each subject has 324 measures: cortical volume (_CV), surface area (_SA), thickness average (_TA) and SD (_TS), and subcortical volume (_SV).

  • All 870 scans are 3T; FreeSurfer 7.2.0 (n=769), 7.4.1 (n=101).
  • Only 101 runs carry a UCSF QC rating; the rest are unrated (qc_rated = False) but kept.
  • Scanner make is known for 371 subjects (Siemens 256, GE 65, Philips 49, Other 1).
  • Matching worked: age 73.2 vs 73.3 years, ~50% female in both groups. Education still differs (15.5 vs 16.6 years).

4. EEG: BrainLat and ds004504

Both EEG datasets run through the same code (prepare_brainlat_eeg.py; prepare_ds004504_eeg.py imports its functions): EEG channels only, average reference, 1–30 Hz band-pass, resample to 256 Hz, first ≤300 s. Features from Welch PSD (2 s windows, 50% overlap): relative power in delta (1–4 Hz), theta (4–8), alpha (8–13) and beta (13–30), globally and per region, theta/alpha ratio, and peak alpha frequency (7–13 Hz).

Channels per scalp region

Regions come from 3-D head coordinates (front > 3 cm, back < −4 cm, lateral > 5 cm = temporal, otherwise central). The two montages differ a lot in density: a BrainLat region averages 19–44 channels, a ds004504 region 1–7. With this rule C3/C4 fall in "temporal", so ds004504 "central" is Cz alone and its central features are noisier than the others.
